Custom Domains

Connect your own domain to your hosted pages

How to connect your domain:

  1. 1. Add your domain to a page below, choosing your verification method (CNAME or A Record).
  2. 2. For CNAME (recommended): Create a CNAME record in your DNS settings pointing to our servers. This is the easiest and most reliable method.
  3. 3. For A Record: Create an A record pointing to our server's IP address: {{ server_ip }}. Note that this IP may change, so CNAME is preferred.
  4. 4. Wait for DNS propagation (this can take up to 48 hours).
  5. 5. We'll automatically verify and activate your domain.
